    Frequently Asked Questions

    How much does house construction cost in Chennai?

    Construction cost depends on the specifications, design, site conditions and the package you select. Our current package rates are listed in the Packages section, and we prepare a project-specific estimate once we understand your plot and requirements.

    What is included in house construction?

    Inclusions depend on the selected package and the project scope. Share your requirements with us and we will explain exactly what is covered before you decide.

    Do you provide 2D house plans?

    Yes, subject to the service scope agreed for the project. Plans are developed around your plot dimensions, requirements and functionality.

    Do you provide 3D elevation designs?

    Yes. 3D elevation and exterior designs help you see the proposed appearance of your home before construction begins.

    Do you undertake renovation?

    Yes. We handle renovation, modification and improvement of existing residential properties.

    Do you construct houses in Tiruvannamalai?

    Yes. Tiruvannamalai is one of our primary service areas, along with Chennai and the wider Tamil Nadu region.

    Can I build a house if I already have a plan?

    Yes. Share your existing plan with us and we will discuss it along with your construction requirements.

    How do I get a construction estimate?

    Send us your location, plot size, number of floors, approximate built-up area and requirements through the form below or on WhatsApp, and our team will get back to you.
